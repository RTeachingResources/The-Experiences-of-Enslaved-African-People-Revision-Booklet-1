Welcome to the QS National History Museum Archive. This revision resource covers Key Issue 1: The Trade in Enslaved African People (1770–1807).

The Transatlantic Slave Trade caused immense human suffering, trauma, and structural destruction across West Africa and the Americas. As historians, we study these events with dignity, academic integrity, and profound respect for the victims and freedom seekers.

Humanizing Language Policy: In line with modern historical practice, this module strictly uses humanizing terminology—referring to captives as "enslaved Africans", captors as "enslavers", and resistance fighters as "freedom seekers".
